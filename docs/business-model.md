# Business Model: Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operations Coordination

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8341`
- ISCO-08: `8341`
- Occupation: Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operators
- Social impact: farm-safety, rural-employment, equipment-uptime

## Customer

- farm contracting crews
- forestry harvesting crews
- mobile plant equipment operators

## Offer

- crew-roster and field-work scheduling
- equipment service-record logging
- safety-concern flagging
- maintenance-order coordination

## Revenue

- monthly coordination retainer
- per-scheduled-operation fee

## Trust Controls

- no equipment-operation/movement decision is ever finalized by this actor, and an operator's on-site safety judgment is never overridden — this actor coordinates scheduling/logistics only
- registered operator and registered equipment basis required before any proposal
- safety-concern flags always escalate to human review; the governor never resolves a safety concern itself
- maintenance orders above the cost-escalation threshold always require human sign-off
- coordination and audit records are auditable, not editable
